## 🌟 What Is SmartShot?

**SmartShot** is an innovative smart-device system consisting of:

- A **smart shot cup** with built-in measurement sensors  
- A **mobile app** that receives the data  
- Real-time tracking of every drink you take  

Our mission is to make drinking **safer, smarter, and more informed**.

---

## 🚀 Features

- Real-time drink tracking  
- Measurement sensors inside the cup  
- Bluetooth Low Energy communication  
- Interactive mobile app dashboard  
- Session history and insights  

---

## 📱 How It Works

1. You pour a shot  
2. Cup measures the volume  
3. Data is sent instantly via Bluetooth  
4. App displays your statistics  

It’s simple, fast, and surprisingly fun.
