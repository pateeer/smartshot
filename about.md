## Who We Are
We are a team of four students working together on **SmartShot**, an innovative device that brings smart technology into social drinking.  
Our mission is to design a reliable and user-friendly way to track alcohol consumption in real time, helping people stay informed, safe, and in control.

## Our Team

### 👤 Patrik Pišta – Developer & System Designer
Patrik focuses on the technical design of SmartShot, backend logic, and the integration between the hardware device and the mobile application. He brings experience with modern software technologies and system architecture.

### 👤 Kristián Gerhát – Hardware & Embedded Systems Specialist
Kristián is responsible for the electronic components, sensor calibration, and data measurement inside the SmartShot cup. He ensures the device collects accurate, reliable, and consistent drinking-session data.

### 👤 Marek Podolský – Mobile App & UI/UX Developer
Marek designs and develops the SmartShot mobile application. He works on intuitive UI, data visualization, and seamless Bluetooth connectivity with the smart cup.

### 👤 Erik Mokrán – Hardware Prototyping & Testing
Erik focuses on building and testing the physical prototype of the SmartShot cup. He manages 3D modeling, material selection, and device durability testing to ensure the product is practical and ready for real-world use.

## Our Mission
To create an intelligent, practical tool that helps users track alcohol intake easily and responsibly—without disrupting the social experience.

## Our Values
- **Innovation** – Bringing modern tech into everyday life  
- **Responsibility** – Helping users drink more safely  
- **Reliability** – Accurate measurements and stable software  
- **User Experience** – Simple, clean, and enjoyable design
